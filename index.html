02 / HOW I THINK

Understand first.
Choose with purpose.

I investigate the workflow before choosing the technology. Sometimes the useful answer is a clearer process; sometimes it is a system that needs to be built.

Explore each step to see the questions I bring to a problem.

01Discover the business problem

What is getting in the way, who is affected and what would a useful result look like?

02Understand users, systems and processes

Listen to users, map the current workflow and identify dependencies, constraints and information gaps.

03Assess feasible solution options

Compare process changes, existing system capabilities and new development against value, cost and risk.

04Translate needs into requirements and design

Make business rules, roles, use cases and acceptance criteria clear enough for shared decisions.

05Implement, test and validate

Configure or build, trace requirements and test real user journeys with stakeholders.

06Support users and improve

Provide guidance, investigate issues and use feedback and agreed measures to refine the solution.

A QUESTION BEFORE A SOLUTION

Does this problem actually need AI?

My illustrative decision framework — not a deployed banking solution or an AI application I have professionally implemented.

Start with process improvement.

Clarify ownership, handoffs and approval steps before introducing another tool.

Every option needs a value check. For AI, assess data readiness, privacy and governance, human oversight, cost and measurable benefits before a pilot.
